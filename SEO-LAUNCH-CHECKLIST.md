# SEO launch checklist

- [ ] Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the canonical HTTPS domain before launch. Then verify ownership in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the generated sitemap.
- [ ] Confirm the canonical host, social image preview, sitemap, robots rules, GEO references, and `/.well-known/security.txt` use the registered domain.
- [ ] Complete Google Business Profile with the Software company category, services, photos, and regular posts.
- [ ] Keep business name, address, and contact details consistent anywhere the company is listed.
- [ ] Create and maintain company profiles on LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and GitHub.
- [ ] Evaluate relevant profiles on Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Behance, Dribbble, Crunchbase, and eligible knowledge bases.
- [ ] Review relevant Egyptian business directories and publish useful guest content with legitimate backlinks.
- [ ] Ask clients for reviews on Google and Clutch after successful project delivery.
- [ ] Publish case-study videos on YouTube where client permission allows.
- [ ] Maintain a monthly content calendar; review article accuracy and freshness.
- [ ] Replace all bracketed contact, social, project, team, testimonial, price, and founding-year placeholders before launch.
- [ ] Configure analytics and consent only after the relevant IDs and privacy requirements are confirmed.
- [ ] Verify all canonical, hreflang, JSON-LD, sitemap, robots, and contact details in production.
- [ ] Add the production domain to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, then monitor index coverage and rich-result reports.
